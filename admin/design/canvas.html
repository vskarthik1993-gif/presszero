01 The Mark

One emblem, two surfaces

The 3D golden ring is the sole PressZero symbol. It is a rendered object with a fixed geometry and finish — never redrawn, recoloured beyond the approved finishes, or flattened. It lives on two surfaces: obsidian for expressive, primary use and paper for documents and correspondence.

06 The Voice Signal

A single line that speaks

A thin gold waveform is the brand's secondary device — a divider, a footer rule, a signature flourish. It stays fine-lined and mostly flat, spiking only at its centre, echoing a calm voice.

10 Misuse

Protect the mark
